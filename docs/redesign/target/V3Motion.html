Motion

Few transitions, all ease-out, 250 ms unless the distance needs more. Nothing moves on its own except the hero deal and Play. With reduced motion on, everything changes at once.

Deal

Hero, once on load

Each capture slides out from under the latest to its date. 450 ms ease-out, 35 ms apart, newest first. The change bars then grow from the axis, 250 ms ease-out.

Lift

Hero, hover or focus a sliver

That capture rises 12 px, its date appears above it, its change bar turns amber. 250 ms ease-out. Try it on the hero board.

Open

Click a capture or a sample

The picked capture grows into the stage as a shared-element view transition; the hero text fades and the address field settles into the header. 350 ms ease-out.

Divider

Slider compare

Follows the pointer one to one, no easing. Arrow keys move it in 5% steps, 250 ms ease-out. Picking another change returns it to 50%.

Select

Ribbon, change list, arrows

The shaded span and the cyan and amber hairlines move to the new pair, 250 ms ease-out. The pictures crossfade, 250 ms. The text diff swaps at once.

Play

Play button or Space

Single mode. Each capture crossfades in over 250 ms and holds 1.2 s; the playhead slides linearly to the next date.

Arrive

Live run

A capture appears as a text outline (fade 250 ms); its change bar grows when it has been scored. The progress bar eases to the new width.

Menu

Focus the address field

Samples open below the field: opacity and a 4 px rise, 150 ms ease-out. Closing is instant.
